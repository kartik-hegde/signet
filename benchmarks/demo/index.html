        The division of labor

        WebMCP is the speed. Signet is the trust.

        Native tools expose application capabilities. Signet adds reusable authorization, idempotency, verification, cancellation, and observability around consequential calls.

        — correctness — honesty

        Safety values come from deterministic fault injection and an independent database oracle. The durable-store arm demonstrates Signet with an application-owned store; that adapter is not currently shipped by Signet.
